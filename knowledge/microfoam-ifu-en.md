# MicroFoam — Instructions for Use (English)

Device name: MicroFoam (MicroFoam™), by BioChange.
For veterinary use only.
Document: LB132 E Rev01.

## Description

MicroFoam is a scaffold for pocket filling and bone augmentation of periodontal defects in dogs in clinical settings. It is a rectangular (4 mm x 6 mm x 0.8 mm) 3D stable structure of dry foam made of enzymatically cross-linked gelatin. MicroFoam is packed as a sterile unit for single use.

## Indications for use

MicroFoam serves as a scaffold for filling and augmentation of periodontal pockets following closed root planing, in dogs.

## Warnings

- MicroFoam is for veterinary use under the care of a healthcare professional.
- MicroFoam is intended for single use only. Do not re-sterilize or reuse.
- Do not use MicroFoam if the sterile package is open, damaged, or past its expiration date.
- Avoid using on dogs with sensitivity to gelatin or porcine-derived materials.
- It is recommended to ensure that the tissue is free of infection and debris before applying MicroFoam.
- MicroFoam has not been tested on patients with immunodeficient systems or with systemic conditions compromising immune response.
- It is important to read and understand the following instructions prior to clinical use.

## Storage and shipping conditions

- Store in a clean, dry environment at room temperature (15-25°C).
- The product is shipped at ambient temperature.

## Side effects

- Possible complications with any non-surgical procedure in the oral and maxillofacial region include: infection, inflammation, flap failure, perforation, abscess formation, bone loss, pain, soft tissue irregularities, and complications associated with the use of anesthesia.
- Incompatibility reactions with MicroFoam cannot be completely excluded.

## Instructions for use

Prior to use, the clinician should be familiar with the clinical procedure.
Scaling and root planing should be performed at the intended site before MicroFoam placement.
The intended site should be free of plaque, calculus, and debris.

1. Measure the intended site with a probe. Measure the depth and the width of the defect.
2. Subtract 1mm from each of the measurements to determine the size of the MicroFoam needed, as the MicroFoam will expand slightly once in the pocket.
3. Open the packing. Ensure that you do not damage the MicroFoam unit during package opening.
4. Cut the dry MicroFoam to the predetermined size using sharp scissors.
5. Place the MicroFoam into the prepared site using small tweezers.
6. Ensure the MicroFoam fits the defect and is not protruding from the gingival margin.
7. After placement, it is recommended to apply gentle digital pressure on the site for a few seconds to ensure the MicroFoam stays in place.

## Handling

- The product is supplied sterile and should be considered sterile unless the package has been opened or damaged. Use accepted aseptic technique when handling the product.
- Any remaining/unused product should be discarded according to local regulations.
