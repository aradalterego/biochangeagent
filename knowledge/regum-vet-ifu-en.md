# ReGum Vet — Instructions for Use (English)

Device name: ReGum Vet (ReGum™ Vet), by BioChange.
For veterinary use only.
Document: LB70 E Rev.06.

## Description

ReGum Vet is a scaffold for the filling and augmentation of bony and periodontal defects in dogs in a clinical setting. It is a 3D stable structure of dry foam, made of enzymatically cross-linked gelatin. ReGum Vet is packed as a sterile unit for single use.

|---|---|
| ReGum Vet square shape | Square-shaped dry scaffold with dimensions: L 9 mm x W 9 mm x H 2.5 mm |
| ReGum Vet cone shape | Cone-shaped dry scaffold with dimensions: D 8 mm x H 12 mm |

\* Size relates to a soaked scaffold.

## Indications for use

ReGum Vet is a scaffold for the filling and augmentation of bony and periodontal defects in dogs, in a clinical setting.

## Warnings

- ReGum Vet is for veterinary use under the care of a healthcare professional.
- ReGum Vet is intended for single use only. Do not re-sterilize or reuse.
- Do not use ReGum Vet if the sterile package is open, damaged, or past its expiration date.
- Avoid using on dogs with sensitivity to gelatin or porcine-derived materials.
- Avoid using on dogs with local or medical conditions considered unsuitable for oral surgery.
- It is recommended to ensure that the tissue is free of infection before applying ReGum Vet.
- ReGum Vet has not been tested in patients with compromised immune function or with systemic conditions impacting immune response.
- It is important to read and understand the following instructions prior to clinical use.

## Storage and shipping conditions

- Store in a clean, dry environment at room temperature (15-25°C).
- The product is shipped at ambient temperature.

## Side effects

- Possible complications with any surgery in the oral and maxillofacial region include: infection, flap slough, perforation, abscess formation, bone loss, pain, soft tissue irregularities, and complications associated with the use of anesthesia.
- Incompatibility reactions with ReGum Vet cannot be completely excluded.

## Instructions for use

Prior to use, the clinician should be familiar with the clinical procedure.
Scaling and root planning should be performed at the intended site before ReGum Vet placement.

1. ReGum Vet units can be used dry or wet.
2. Open the pouch packing. Make sure not to damage the ReGum Vet unit during the package opening.
3. For wetting: (a) Prepare a sterile receptacle with sterile saline or Lactated Ringer's Solution. The amount of liquid should be enough to cover the product units (estimated 1-3 ml). (b) Wet the ReGum Vet unit in the prepared sterile liquid.
4. The scaffold can be adjusted to the desired size before placement, if needed. A dry scaffold cannot be trimmed.
5. Place the ReGum Vet in the prepared site. Optional: Use Dental Plugger for gentle condensation of the ReGum Vet in the defect. There is no need for an additional membrane.
6. Secure ReGum Vet to its place using overlying (periosteal) sutures to prevent motion and migration of the material from the treated site.

## Handling

- The product is supplied sterile and should be considered sterile unless the package has been opened or damaged. Use accepted aseptic technique when handling the product.
- Any remaining/unused product should be discarded according to local regulations.
