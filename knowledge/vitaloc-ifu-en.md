# vitaloc — Instructions for Use (English)

Device name: vitaloc (vitaloc™), by BioChange.
For veterinary use only.
Document: LB111 E Rev05.

## Description

vitaloc can be used to control venous and/or arterial hemorrhage on a variety of procedures, including but not limited to: teeth extractions, oral mass removal, maxillectomies/mandibulectomies, soft tissue biopsies, enucleations, and limb surgeries or traumatic injuries. vitaloc consists of gelatin and crosslinking enzyme powders. vitaloc is packed in a tube, wrapped in an aluminum package, and contains 500 milligrams of powder. The product is sterilized by e-beam irradiation.

## Mode of action

vitaloc is a powder mix of gelatin and crosslinking enzyme that acts as a hemostat for controlling medium-severe bleeding during surgical procedures or traumatic injuries. Once applied on a bleeding site the gelatin crosslinking is initiated. The gelatin is crosslinked into a stable matrix that mechanically stops the bleeding at the injured site, regardless of the patient's coagulation status. vitaloc consists of biodegradable materials that are gradually absorbed by the body over time.

## Indications for use

vitaloc is indicated for controlling medium-severe bleeding.

## Warnings

- vitaloc is for veterinary use under the care of a healthcare professional.
- vitaloc is intended for single use only. Do not re-sterilize or reuse.
- Do not use vitaloc if the package is open, damaged, or past its expiration date.
- Potential for embolization and death may exist. Do not inject or place vitaloc inside blood vessels.
- Avoid using on dogs with sensitivity to gelatin, maltodextrin, transglutaminase (TG), or porcine-derived materials.
- In cases where there is multifocal bleeding from small blood vessels, it is recommended to limit the use to an area of 2×2 cm² per vial.
- vitaloc is not intended as a substitute for meticulous surgical technique and proper application of ligatures or other conventional procedures for hemostasis.
- Use caution when applying in direct contact with large blood vessels as potential occlusion may exist.
- The efficacy and safety of vitaloc in ophthalmic procedures have not been established.
- It is recommended to ensure that the tissue is free of infection before applying vitaloc.
- It is important to read and understand the following instructions prior to clinical use.
- Symptoms of allergy, asthma symptoms, or breathing difficulties may occur if inhaled.

The following adverse events have been reported for other hemostatic agents and may apply to the use of vitaloc:

- Paralysis and nerve damage have been reported when hemostatic agents are used in or in proximity to foramina in bone, areas of bony confine, the spinal cord, and/with laminectomy, reports of paralysis have also been received in connection with other procedures.
- Compression of the brain and spinal cord resulting from the accumulation of sterile fluid has been observed.

## Storage and handling

- Store in a clean, dry environment at refrigeration conditions (2-8°C).
- The product is shipped at ambient temperature.

## Side effects

- Possible complications with any surgery include but are not limited to, infection, flap slough, perforation, abscess formation, bone loss, pain, soft tissue irregularities, wound dehiscence, seroma, hematoma and complications associated with the use of anesthesia.
- Incompatibility reactions with vitaloc cannot be completely excluded.

## Instructions for use

vitaloc should be used according to the following instructions:

1. Open the aluminum package using scissors and remove the vitaloc tube.
2. Gently tap the vitaloc tube several times to release powder aggregates.
3. Visually inspect the tube and its contents for integrity or discoloration. The powder is white to light yellow and homogenous. If damaged, do not use it.
4. Prepare the application site by identifying the source of bleeding. Remove excess blood by blotting, wiping, or suctioning. It is important to apply vitaloc directly to the site of the active bleeding.
5. Open the tube cap and apply a liberal amount of the powder as close to the source of the bleeding as possible. Deep wounds may require an equally deep application of the powder. * It is recommended to use the entire contents of the vial for maximum effectiveness.
6. Place sterile gauze over the powder and apply pressure for 4 minutes.
7. Carefully remove the gauze and inspect the treated site.
8. If bleeding continues, repeat steps 5-6 as needed.
9. Once hemostasis is achieved, there is no need to remove or irrigate the excess material and the surgical procedure can continue as planned.

## Handling

- The product is supplied sterile and should be considered sterile unless the package has been opened or damaged. Use accepted aseptic technique when handling the product. Use protective eyewear or a face screen, and handle with protective gloves.
- Any remaining/unused product should be discarded according to local regulations.
