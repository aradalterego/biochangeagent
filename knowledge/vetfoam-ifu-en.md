# VetFoam — Instructions for Use (English)

Device name: VetFoam (VetFoam™), by BioChange.
For veterinary use only.
Document: LB92 E Rev06.

## Description

VetFoam is indicated for controlling Urethral Sphincter Mechanism Incompetence (USMI) in female dogs. VetFoam consists of a gelatin powder and a cross-linking enzyme. VetFoam is provided in a dry powder form in a prefilled syringe, which is mixed at the point of care, together with air and sterile saline, using a second syringe, to generate an injectable foam.

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| VetFoam syringe | 5 ml syringe filled with 800 mg powder |
| Wings syringe | Inject8™ syringe with wings grip |

## Indications for use

VetFoam is indicated for controlling Urethral Sphincter Mechanism Incompetence (USMI) in female dogs.

## Warnings

- VetFoam is for veterinary use only, under the care of a healthcare professional.
- VetFoam is for single use only. Do not re-sterilize or re-use.
- Do not use VetFoam if the sterile package is open, damaged, or past its expiration date.
- Avoid using on dogs with sensitivity to gelatin, transglutaminase (TG), maltodextrin, or porcine-derived materials.
- Avoid using on dogs with local or medical conditions considered unsuitable for surgery.
- It is recommended to ensure that the tissue is free of infection before applying VetFoam.
- VetFoam has not been tested in patients with compromised immune function or with systemic conditions impacting immune response.
- It is important to read and understand the following instructions prior to clinical use.
- Symptoms of allergy, asthma symptoms, or breathing difficulties may occur if inhaled.

## Storage and shipping conditions

- Store in a clean, dry environment at refrigeration conditions (2-8°C).
- The product is shipped at ambient temperature.

## Side effects

- Possible complications with any surgery include: infection, flap slough, perforation, abscess formation, pain, soft tissue irregularities, and complications associated with the use of anesthesia.
- Incompatibility reactions with VetFoam cannot be completely excluded.
- Temporary urinary obstruction may occur.
- Hemorrhage at injection sites may occur.

## Instructions for use — VetFoam preparation steps

1. Work with gloves to keep the VetFoam syringe sterile.
2. Warm a sterile saline bag for the procedure to 37°C (for the cystoscope and mixing). Avoid injecting VetFoam with the cystoscope temperature below 25°C.
3. Open the aluminum package of the VetFoam powder syringe.
4. VetFoam syringe with the powder is closed with a red cap-luer connector – red cap.
5. Open slightly the red cap attached to the syringe with the powder so it is attached loosely to the syringe. Bring the plunger to the 4 ml line.
6. Gently tap with your finger on the top part of the VetFoam syringe several times to release any powder aggregates. Bring the plunger up to the 2 ml line.
7. Remove the red caps from the VetFoam syringe, and re-attach the luer connector to the VetFoam syringe. You now have the VetFoam syringe with a luer connector ready for mixing.
8. Fill the Wings syringe with 2.2 ml sterile saline from the warm saline bag.

## Instructions for use — Final preparation steps

Start these steps when ready to inject.

9. The cystoscope should be at a temperature of 37°C.
10. The dedicated needle for bladder injection (BONEE 1.7 mm needle – Coloplast) should be placed in the desired location.
11. Connect the saline filled (2.2 ml) Wings syringe with the VetFoam syringe. Keep the two connected syringes horizontal until mixing.
12. Push the plunger of the saline Wings syringe all the way into the powder-containing syringe, and push the plunger of the powder syringe halfway into the liquid syringe. Repeat this halfway-mixing 4 times.
13. Push the plungers all the way between the two syringes back and forth rapidly for 30 seconds, until a uniform foam is formed. The last plunge should leave the foam in the Wings syringe. *Note: Once the VetFoam foam has formed, crosslinking initiates and viscosity rises.
14. Wait for 3-4 minutes.
15. Push the plungers all the way between the two syringes back and forth rapidly 4 times.
16. Disconnect the VetFoam-containing Wings syringe. The VetFoam in the Wings syringe is now ready for injection.
17. Connect the Wings syringe (with VetFoam) to the dedicated needle for bladder injection.
18. Prefilling of BONEE needle (Priming) can be performed at room temperature before insertion.
19. Inject 2-4 bulges into the submucosa of the proximal urethra. Following each bulge injection, keep the needle in place for about 30 seconds, pull back and place it in the next desired bulge location.
20. Under these conditions, the procedure time frame (from mixing to final bulge injection) is approximately 5 minutes.

## Handling

- The product is supplied sterile and should be considered sterile unless the package has been opened or damaged. Use accepted aseptic technique when handling the product. Use protective eyewear or a face screen, and handle with protective gloves.
- Any remaining/unused product should be discarded according to local regulations.
